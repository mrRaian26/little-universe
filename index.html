A little something from my heart

For you, Mridu ♡

My dear Mridu,

I don't really know where to begin. There are so many things I've wanted to tell you, and somehow, words never seem quite enough.

It's strange how someone can become such a meaningful part of your thoughts without even being beside you. Even though we haven't had the chance to meet in person, your face is one my mind returns to so often.

Sometimes I find myself thinking about your smile, your little habits, your voice, and all those tiny things you might not even notice about yourself. I wish you could see yourself through my eyes, even for just a moment. Perhaps then you'd understand why you are so special to me.

I know I haven't always expressed myself perfectly. But I want you to know that my feelings were never something I wanted to pretend. What I feel for you is sincere, and I hope my actions can show that more clearly than words ever could.

I don't know what life has planned for either of us, or where our paths will lead. But I genuinely wish to see you happy, to see you achieve everything you've dreamed of, and to have days filled with reasons to smile.

If life ever gives us the opportunity to share little moments together, I'd cherish the ordinary ones just as much as the extraordinary ones: the silly conversations, the quiet moments, the laughter, and everything in between.

Your happiness, your dreams, and your peace matter to me. I don't want you to feel that you owe me anything for the way I feel. I simply want you to know how much you mean to me, and how warmly I wish the very best for you.

I love you, Mridu. Not as a promise you have to answer, or an expectation you have to carry, but as something honest that lives in my heart.

Happy Birthday, my dear. I hope this year brings you beautiful beginnings, gentle days, and all the happiness your heart has been hoping for.

And if I could make one wish for myself, it would be to have the privilege of making you smile on many more birthdays to come.

With all my affection,
Raian ♡
If you give me the opportunity, I'll be alive to make your birthdays for the rest of my life, Mridu.
